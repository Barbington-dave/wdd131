Website Planning Document: Tema Meridian Hub

WDD 131 Project Plan

1. Site Name

1. Site Name: Tema Meridian Hub

Reason: Tema is known as Ghana's Harbour City and sits right near the Prime Meridian (0° longitude) and the Equator, giving the site a clear local and geographic identity.

Optional Domain: temameridianhub.com

2. Site Purpose

The site serves as a local guide and community hub for residents, students, and visitors exploring Tema, Ghana—highlighting key landmarks, coastal and community spots, filterable places, and a form for local suggestions or inquiries.

3. Scenarios

  • Where is the Prime Meridian landmark in Tema located, and what coastal spots or local places can I visit nearby?
  • Which local spots, beaches, or community areas in Tema can I explore, and how can I filter options or submit a recommendation?

4. Color Schema

This site plan uses Atlantic Navy (#0B2545) for headings, header, and footer; Meridian Gold (#D9822B) for accents and borders; and Warm Sand (#F7F9FB) with crisp white (#FFFFFF) and dark slate text (#1B263B) for clean contrast.

5. Typography

  • Headings (h1, h2, h3): Montserrat
  • Body Text & Lists: Open Sans

6. Wireframes

Mobile view stacks sections in a single column; desktop view expands into a multi-column layout with a wider hero and side-by-side feature cards.
